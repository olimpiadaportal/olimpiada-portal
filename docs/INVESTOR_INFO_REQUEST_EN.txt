⚠ TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE CALLING THE BANK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. APPLE DOES NOT PAY IN AZERBAIJANI MANAT. AZN appears nowhere in Apple's
     currency documentation. We are confirming the exact currency Apple accepts
     from the list in App Store Connect (most likely EUR or USD) and will tell
     you which one before you go to the bank — so that no account is opened in
     the wrong currency.

  2. IN AZERBAIJAN, EACH CURRENCY IS A SEPARATE ACCOUNT WITH ITS OWN IBAN.
     The currency is encoded inside the IBAN (944 = AZN, 840 = USD, 978 = EUR).
     We need a FOREIGN-CURRENCY account's IBAN, not the manat one. Giving Apple
     the wrong IBAN means the payment simply never arrives.

When you call the bank, do NOT say "send me our IBAN" — you will be given the
manat account, because that is the one everything else runs through.
